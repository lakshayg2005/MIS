# Acme Technologies - Employee Transport Policy
Version: 1
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Grade and distance
Employees in grade L3 and above are eligible for office transport. Employees in grades L1 and L2 are eligible only if their home is more than 8 km from the office. Interns are not eligible for transport.

### 1.2 Shift-based eligibility
Employees rostered on a shift that starts before 6:00 AM or ends after 8:00 PM are eligible for transport irrespective of grade or distance.

## 2. Booking

### 2.1 Cut-off timings
A cab or shuttle seat must be booked at least 4 hours before the shift start (pickup) or shift end (drop). Requests inside the 4 hour window are treated as ad-hoc trips and need approval under section 5.

### 2.2 Cancellation
A booking can be cancelled free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. A later cancellation is treated as a no-show.

## 3. Night shift and safety

### 3.1 Security escort
A security escort is mandatory for every woman employee on drops after 8:00 PM. The transport desk assigns the escort automatically when the drop is booked. The employee does not need to request it.

### 3.2 Night drop timings
Drops between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM are operated only on fixed routes. The last drop of the night leaves the office at 1:00 AM.

### 3.3 Safety reporting
Any safety concern during a trip must be reported to the transport desk helpline immediately. The driver is not allowed to change the route without informing the employee.

## 4. Weekends and holidays

### 4.1 Weekend transport
Weekend pickups and drops are available on Saturday and Sunday only with the reporting manager's approval, raised at least 24 hours in advance.

### 4.2 Public holidays
Transport on a declared public holiday is provided only for employees in critical support rosters listed by the department head.

## 5. Ad-hoc trips and approvals

### 5.1 Ad-hoc trip approval
An ad-hoc trip (outside the roster or inside the booking cut-off) is approved by the reporting manager. Approval must be recorded before the trip starts.

### 5.2 Airport trips
Airport pickups and drops need approval from the department head. The reporting manager cannot approve an airport trip.

### 5.3 Cost cap
An ad-hoc cab trip is capped at Rs 1,500 per trip. Anything above the cap needs written approval from the finance controller.

## 6. Guests

### 6.1 Guest travel
Guests are not allowed on employee shuttles or cabs. A guest of a visiting client may be transported only with written approval from a vice president.

## 7. No-show and penalties

### 7.1 No-show penalty
The first no-show in a calendar month results in a warning. From the second no-show in the same month, a fee of Rs 200 is charged for each no-show.

### 7.2 Repeated no-shows
After 5 no-shows in a calendar month, transport is suspended for the rest of that month.
